# DutyRoute design decisions

Reading this as a dispatch working surface for a solo property-carrying driver, with paper-log structure and restrained road-sign color. ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

The user authorized completing the project without stopping. Apply antislop during implementation; skip its optional installation wizard and use project-specific design judgment without an approval gate.

- Deep teal anchors the workspace; amber indicates the main planning action and route stops.
- Paper-white log sheets keep the four-row grid legible and printable, matching the supplied reference.
- A narrow trip panel beside the route prioritizes editing and inspection in the same viewport.
- Segoe UI is a familiar Windows system face; tabular numbers keep clocks and log totals aligned.
- Spacious map/results and denser remarks reflect their different information needs.
- No decorative imagery, profile photos, invented metrics, marketing sections, or theme toggle.
- Light mode matches the supplied paper log and remains readable when printed.
- The repeated connected duty line is the product motif and carries actual schedule data.
- Small corner radii group controls without making technical tables look like promotional cards.
- Hover/focus feedback is sufficient; no entrance animation obscures the working surface.
